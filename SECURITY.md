# Security

## Found a security problem? Please tell us first.

If you think you have found a way to break into this site, read other people's private information, post as someone else,
or make the site show harmful content, please tell us privately before telling anyone else.

- Email: **security@YOUR-DOMAIN.example** (the site owner replaces this address before launch)
- Please include: the page address, what you did, what you saw, and what you expected.
- We will answer within 7 days, keep you informed, and credit you if you like.
- Please do not read or change other people's data, do not run automated attacks that slow the site down, and do not
  publish details until we have had a reasonable chance (90 days at most) to fix the problem.

We cannot offer money for reports. We do say thank you.

## What the site does to protect you

- Everything written by visitors (Opinions, when it is on) is shown as plain text, never as code.
- Accounts are handled by Supabase; passwords are never stored by this website. Writing needs a confirmed email address.
- The site stores no tracking cookies of its own. See the privacy page for what advertising partners may add.
- The Content-Security-Policy added at deploy time allows pictures and connections from this site only (plus Google Fonts for the text and, when ads are switched on, the ad vendors).

## For the owner

The full audit and the launch-day checklist are in `audit/security-audit.md` in the project folder (not published).
Publish `.well-known/security.txt` after replacing every `YOUR-...` placeholder in it.
