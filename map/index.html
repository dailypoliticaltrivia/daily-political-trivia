          About this map

          The year shows the Congress in session on July 1 of that year. Seats that changed hands during the year list every holder, with dates. Before 1913, state legislatures chose senators, and some Senate seats sat empty for months.

          District lines are redrawn after each census and sometimes in between, when courts strike a map down or a legislature redraws mid-decade. Turn on Redistricting to see the previous Congress's lines as dashed outlines. Districts whose shape changed are hatched.

          Keyboard: arrow keys on the timeline move one year (Shift moves ten). Every state and district is a button, and Escape goes back. Sources and photo credits are on the Credits page.
