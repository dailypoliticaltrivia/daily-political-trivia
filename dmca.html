Copyright policy

We respect copyright. Here is how to tell us about a problem and what we do about it.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Our approach

Daily Political Trivia shows politician portraits that are in the public domain or openly licensed (credited on the Credits page), data from public records, and its own games, code, question banks and puzzle sets. Visitors cannot upload or post anything to the site. If you are a copyright owner and something here is yours, you can ask us to remove it.

How to contact us

Email dmca@dailypoiticaltrivia.com. It reaches the person who runs the site, an individual in Maryland, doing business as Daily Political Trivia. This site does not host content uploaded by visitors and has not registered a designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office; requests are handled directly by email. Other complaints (privacy, wrong answers, a person shown in a game, anything not about copyright) go to legal@dailypoiticaltrivia.com or corrections@dailypoiticaltrivia.com.

What to include

A clear message makes it quick to act. Please include:

  1. your name and contact details, and the name of the rights holder if you act for someone else;
  2. what the copyrighted work is (a link to the original is ideal);
  3. where the material appears on our site: the web address (URL) of the page, and which game and which photograph;
  4. a statement that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act for the owner, and that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner or the law;
  5. your signature (typing your full name is fine).

Please consider whether the use could be fair use or covered by a licence (for example Creative Commons) before writing, and only send a request if you are the rights holder or authorised by them. Knowingly false claims can have legal consequences for the sender.

What we do

  • We read every message and reply promptly to say what we did.
  • For a portrait or photo in a game, we remove or replace it, or correct the credit if the credit was wrong or missing.
  • For text, code or data of yours that you say we copied, we review it and remove or rewrite it if the claim is valid.
  • If a request is unclear we may ask you for the missing details before acting.

Pictured people

If you are a person shown in a game (a current or former public official) and you object to a photograph, or something about how you are described is wrong, write to corrections@dailypoiticaltrivia.com. See also "About the people shown" in the Terms of Use.

Credits and licences

Photo credits and open-data attributions are on the Credits page. If a credit is wrong or missing, tell us and we will fix it. See also the Terms of Use.
