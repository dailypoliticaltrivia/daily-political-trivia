Data sources

  • Members of Congress, 1789 to today: the Biographical Directory of the United States Congress and the unitedstates project (public domain / CC0 data, current and historical legislators, committees).
  • Facts about people, offices and dates: Wikidata, released under CC0, and public-domain works of the U.S. government.
  • Photographs: official U.S. government portraits (public domain) and files from Wikimedia Commons under public-domain or Creative Commons licences, credited above. We only crop and resize them.
  • Election results: reported by official state sources and major news organisations, then checked by hand against two or more sources.
  • Questions and answers: written for Daily Political Trivia.

Facts themselves are not copyrighted, but we thank the volunteers who gather them. Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata are projects of the Wikimedia Foundation; we are not affiliated with it.

About the people shown
