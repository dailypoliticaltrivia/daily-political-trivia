Privacy policy

What this site collects (almost nothing), who else sees your visit, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who we are

Daily Political Trivia ("we", "the site") is run by an individual in Maryland, doing business as Daily Political Trivia. For privacy questions or requests write to privacy@dailypoiticaltrivia.com. This policy is part of our Terms of Use.

The short version

  • Daily Political Trivia is a set of free daily political games and an interactive Congress Map. You can use all of it without an account. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no comments and no forms.
  • The site does not collect your name, email address or any other personal information, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your game progress, results and settings (such as your theme choice) are saved only on your own device.
  • The companies that host the site and its domain can see technical data such as your IP address when your browser asks them for a page, as explained below.
  • We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

  • When you play: nothing that identifies you reaches us. The site is a set of static pages and data files. We run no server of our own that stores personal data, and the games work out your score in your browser.
  • Access logs: the company that hosts the site (see below) keeps ordinary access logs, such as IP address, browser type, page requested and time. We do not receive those logs in readable form, do not build profiles from them and do not combine them with anything else.
  • If you write to us: your message and your email address, which we use only to answer you and to keep a record of the request. We do not add you to any list.

What your browser stores (localStorage)

The site saves small pieces of information in your own browser so it works the way you left it. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • your light or dark theme choice;
  • your game progress, past results and today's results;
  • your sound and animation settings, display options (such as Caucus Sort's faces-only mode), which questions you have already seen and the last location you chose in Capitol Climb's Explore mode.

You can clear all of this in your browser settings. The games then start fresh and your saved progress and settings are lost. We do not set cookies ourselves.

Other companies that see your visit

Because the site is made of pieces delivered by different companies, each of them receives the ordinary technical data a browser sends with any request (your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time). They handle it under their own privacy terms:

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub keeps server logs for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Domain name and security: our domain name and email forwarding are provided by Cloudflare, which may also see requests to the domain and keeps security logs. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Fonts: the typefaces (Newsreader and Public Sans) are files stored with the site itself, so no font company receives any request from your browser.
  • Photos and data: the portraits and data used in the games are files stored with the site itself, not loaded from other companies' servers. The credits page links to the original sources, and if you click one you leave this site and that site's own privacy practices apply.

We do not receive any of this data from them in readable form, and we do not combine it with anything else.

Cookies and similar technology

We do not set cookies and we do not use advertising or tracking technology. The only storage we use is the localStorage described above, which is needed for the site to remember your theme and your games. Our hosting and security providers may use technically necessary storage to keep the service safe.

Analytics and advertising

We do not use analytics and the site shows no advertising. If that ever changes we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything is switched on.

How long we keep things

  • Emails to us: only as long as needed to deal with your request and keep a record of it, then deleted.
  • Server and security logs at our providers: kept by them under their own policies.
  • Data in your browser: until you clear it.

Children

Daily Political Trivia is made for a general audience of teens and adults who follow civics and politics. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The site has no accounts, no sign-up and no messaging, and it asks for nothing. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to privacy@dailypoiticaltrivia.com and we will delete it. Parents and guardians: because progress is stored only in the browser on your device, you can remove it at any time by clearing the site's data.

Your rights and choices

Because we hold almost no personal information, there is usually nothing to access or delete beyond any emails you sent us. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask for a copy by writing to privacy@dailypoiticaltrivia.com. We will not treat you worse for using these rights, and we will answer within 45 days.

  • United States, including Maryland: depending on where you live (for example under the Maryland Online Data Privacy Act or the laws of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states) you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of your personal data and to opt out of its sale, of targeted advertising and of profiling. We do not sell or share personal data, do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and do not process sensitive personal data. If we refuse a request you may appeal by replying to our answer and may contact your state attorney general (in Maryland, the Consumer Protection Division of the Office of the Attorney General).
  • Outside the United States: the site is intended for people in the United States. If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland you may have rights to access, correct, delete and port your data, to object to its use and to complain to your data protection authority. Any data we hold, such as an email you sent us, is processed in the United States.

Security

The site is delivered over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure. To report a security problem write to security@dailypoiticaltrivia.com. If a breach ever affected personal information we hold, we would notify the people affected and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

We will update this page when the site changes and show the date at the top. For material changes we will also post a notice on the site.
