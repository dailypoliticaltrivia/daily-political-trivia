Accessibility statement

Daily Political Trivia should work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Our goal

We aim for the site and its games to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. This is a goal we work toward, not a guarantee that every page or game already meets it, and the site has not had a formal accessibility audit.

What we do

  • Pages use headings, landmarks and labelled controls so assistive technology can navigate them, and a "skip to content" link.
  • We aim to make the games work with a keyboard alone, with visible focus.
  • Text and controls are designed for sufficient colour contrast in both the light and dark themes, and text can be enlarged without losing content.
  • Pages are built to work on phones and small screens.
  • The site has no advertising, no auto-playing video and no pop-ups.

Known limits

  • We have not yet completed keyboard and screen-reader testing of every game, so some games may be harder to use than others. Games with timers (Dark Horse gives 30 seconds a question) can be hard for some players, and we cannot yet offer an untimed mode.
  • Caucus Sort asks you to sort politicians' photographs, so it relies on seeing faces; names are available as text, but the game is harder to play without sight. Tell us if you need another way to play.
  • Some colour cues may be hard to tell apart for people with colour-vision differences. Tell us about any we missed.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site does not work for you, write to accessibility@dailypoiticaltrivia.com and tell us the page address, what you were trying to do and what assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within a week and to offer another way to play or to fix the problem. We review this statement at least once a year.
