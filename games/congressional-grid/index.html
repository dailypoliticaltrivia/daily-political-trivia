The rules

How to play

Each square sits where a row and a column meet. Pick a member of Congress who fits both categories.

  • You have 9 guesses. Every pick uses one, right or wrong.
  • You can't use the same person twice.
  • Each correct pick shows a rarity score from 1 to 100. Members from small states score lower, which is better, because fewer people can name them.
  • Your score is the total of your nine squares. An empty square counts as 100. Lowest score wins; a perfect grid scores 9.
  • A new daily grid appears every day at midnight. Practice grids are unlimited and aren't saved.
