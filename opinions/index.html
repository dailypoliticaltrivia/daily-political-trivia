Opinion Guidelines

  • Argue with ideas, not people.
  • No harassment, hate, threats or doxxing.
  • Be honest about facts and cite sources where you can.
  • No spam or self-promotion.

Use Report on anything that breaks these rules. Moderators may remove content or suspend accounts.
