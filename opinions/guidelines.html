Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old (the one age rule for the whole site, also stated in the Terms of Use and Privacy policy) and give a real email address that you can read. We use it to confirm your account and to reset your password. It is never shown publicly.
  • Choose a display name that is yours or that you are happy to be known by. Do not pretend to be another person or organization.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what is posted from your account. Use a long password you do not use anywhere else.
  • One person, one account. Do not create extra accounts to get around a suspension or a rate limit.

What you can write

  • Argue with ideas, not people. Disagree as sharply as you like. No personal attacks.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or doxxing. Never post someone else's private information.
  • Be honest. Do not knowingly spread false information. Link to sources for factual claims.
  • Write your own work. No plagiarism. Quote briefly and give credit.
  • No spam or undisclosed promotion. Say so if you are paid or have a stake in what you write about.
  • Keep it legal and safe. Nothing illegal, sexually explicit, or that puts anyone in danger.

Ownership and licence

You keep the copyright in what you write. By publishing, you give Stay Informed News a free, non-exclusive licence to display, store and share your piece on the site and in links to it. You can delete your own pieces and comments at any time, and the licence ends for what you delete (copies that others already made or quoted may remain).

Moderation

Anyone signed in can report a piece or comment. Moderators may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules, with or without warning. Suspended accounts can still read the site. To appeal, contact us at legal@dailypoliticaltrivia.com within 30 days. We remove content that infringes copyright when notified under our copyright policy, and repeat infringers lose their accounts. Content that is false and damaging about a real person, harassing, doxxing, or that knowingly spreads false information about how, when or where to vote or about election results can be removed.

Limits

To protect the community from spam, there are limits on how much one account can post per hour. The limits are published in plain numbers and may change.

Views and responsibility

Opinion pieces and comments are the views of their authors, not of Stay Informed News. The site is provided as is, and we do not promise it will always be available.
