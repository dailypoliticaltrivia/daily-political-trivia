Before launch checklist

  1. Site is live on your own domain over https (not only github.io).
  2. Enough original content: Opinions, original game content, About, Privacy, a real contact address.
  3. Privacy policy finished with real details and reviewed by a lawyer.
  4. Ad account approved (AdSense and/or Ad Manager, or a managed partner). Ids pasted into ads-config.js.
  5. Consent tool set up (Google Funding Choices or another certified CMP) and tested from an EU location.
  6. ads.txt contains the real lines and opens at https://your-domain/ads.txt.
  7. Test mode run once after deployment (then turned off again), and ?adsdebug=1 output looks right.
  8. enabled set to true.
